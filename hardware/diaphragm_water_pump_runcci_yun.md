## Summary of vendor description
Cost: 12.99 Eur (x2 pumps).
Working voltage: DC 6-12V, maximum flow: 1.5-1.8L/min
The size of the pump: 95 mm (L) x 47 mm (W) x 36 mm (H). Inlet tube diameter: 5 mm

## Personal Measurements
Resistance of the pump: 12.5 R.

With a source of 12.3V the current is 350 mA.
The effective DC impedance at running speed of the pump is 35 Ohms (12.3V / 0.35A).
So the motor is generating 12.3V - (0.35A * 12.5 Ohms)  = 7.9V of back-EMF.

Note: worth testing the flow at 12.3V.
